# AI Citation Research Workflow

## What this app solves

Traditional rank tracking cannot explain why an AI answer cites a competitor instead of your website. This app repeats real customer questions, records the search and citation evidence, diagnoses where your domain dropped out, and turns the finding into a controlled website experiment.

It answers four practical questions:

1. Was our page retrieved?
2. If retrieved, was it cited?
3. If cited, was our brand mentioned or recommended?
4. What observable difference between the cited page and our page should we test?

## The research path

### Project setup

Provide the target domain, relevant pages you can improve, the audience and market, a seed customer question, and known competitor domains. Saving the project opens Step 1.

### Step 1 — Choose tests

Create 5–10 natural-language prompts covering discovery, comparison, trust, logistics, and booking decisions. Do not rewrite prompts after the baseline; they are the measurement instrument.

### Step 2 — Run the baseline

Run each priority prompt 3–5 times on configured platforms. The output records generated search queries, retrieved pages, cited pages, and brand mentions. A repeated pattern is stronger evidence than one answer.

### Step 3 — Fix a citation gap

Start with a High-priority opportunity. Confirm the target URL, competitor citation, funnel stage, and observed difference. Approve one experiment and make one controlled change to the named page—for example, a direct answer block, clearer entity context, first-hand expert evidence, structured comparison, or better supporting facts.

### Step 4 — Measure progress

After the changed page can be recrawled, rerun the same prompts on the same platforms with the same run count. Compare retrieval, citation, mention, and recommendation frequency. Mark the experiment Validated, Inconclusive, or Archived.

## What to tell a manager

“We are testing why AI systems cite competitor pages for customer questions that matter to our business. We establish a repeatable baseline, identify the stage where our page loses visibility, make one evidence-led page change, and rerun the same tests. Success means a repeated improvement in retrieval, citation, or recommendation—not a single favourable answer.”

## Research limitation

The app finds observable patterns and creates testable hypotheses. It cannot prove that one page feature is a proprietary AI ranking factor.
